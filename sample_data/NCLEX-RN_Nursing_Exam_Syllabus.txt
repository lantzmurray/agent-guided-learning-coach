NCLEX-RN (National Council Licensure Examination - Registered Nurse)

EXAM OVERVIEW:
The NCLEX-RN is a computerized adaptive test that determines whether a candidate is safe and competent to practice as an entry-level registered nurse. All U.S. state boards of nursing require passage for licensure.

EXAM FORMAT:
- Question types: Multiple choice, multiple response, fill-in-the-blank, drag-and-drop
- Minimum: 75 questions, Maximum: 265 questions
- Time limit: 5 hours
- Passing standard determined by psychometric analysis

INCORPORATING THE NURSING PROCESS:
1. Assessment - Data collection, history taking, physical examination
2. Diagnosis - Identify actual or at-risk nursing diagnoses
3. Planning - Set goals, outcomes, and interventions
4. Implementation - Execute nursing interventions
5. Evaluation - Assess effectiveness of care
